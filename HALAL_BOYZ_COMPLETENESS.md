The decisive evidence: a grep for stub vocabulary (`TODO|FIXME|placeholder|stub|not implemented`) hits 20 sites — **every one is an anti-stub guardrail**, not a stub:

A codebase whose tests **prohibit** stubs is the opposite of a stubbed codebase.
