## The one ambiguity, and how it was resolved

The card says "branch-level OPTIONAL `terminalLocationId`". This config's
`branches` section is a **map**, not a list, and already holds a flat
`defaultBranchId`. Two readings are therefore possible, and the repo cannot
settle it: `branches.terminalLocationId` (flat, matching `defaultBranchId`) or
`branches.<branchId>.terminalLocationId` (per-branch, matching the multi-site
case in the expansion).

Both are accepted and type-checked; neither is read. Rejecting the flat form
would have broken the precedent set by the section's own existing key, and
rejecting the nested form would have made the multi-site requirement
unrepresentable. Recorded as **D-013 (ASSUMED)** with the verification step:
the owner rules on the shape before P1 writes a reader; whichever shape loses,
what remains is deleting validation code, never migrating data.
