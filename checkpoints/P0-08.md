file that **differs from what the registry would produce** fails. `--check` never
writes, so the gate cannot repair its own drift.

## What an ACTIVE row will do (unexercised today)

The probe reads and then writes a tenant-B document path as the current (tenant
A) principal and passes only on `permission-denied`; any other outcome fails with
a message distinguishing "leak" from "could not check" (no emulator, no sign-in).
It is intentionally unexercised: with 0 ACTIVE rows there is nothing to run, and
faking an ACTIVE row would mean asserting a denial the rules do not make.
